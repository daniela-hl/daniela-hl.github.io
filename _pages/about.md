## Academic position and background

I am an Assistant Professor in the Operations Department at the Kellogg School of Management. Before joining Kellogg, I was an Assistant Professor of Mathematics at William & Mary for 1.5 years. I obtained my Ph.D. in Operations Research from Georgia Tech in December 2021, under the supervision of [Prof. Siva Theja Maguluri](https://sites.google.com/site/sivatheja/){:target="_blank"}. 


## Work-life balance

When I am not working, you will probably find me surrounded by dogs: my own or the dogs at the [Evanston Animal Shelter](https://evanstonanimalshelter.net/){:target="_blank"} (EAS). I enjoy dog training, and I am very passionate about volunteering at EAS (#adoptdontshop). As a volunteer, I walk dogs a couple of times a week, I am an adoption counselor, and I help with transportation and some media content.
