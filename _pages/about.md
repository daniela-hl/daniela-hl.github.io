## Academic position and background

I am an Assistant Professor in the Operations Department at the Kellogg School of Management. Before joining Kellogg, I was an Assistant Professor of Mathematics at William & Mary for 1.5 years. I obtained my Ph.D. in Operations Research from Georgia Tech in December 2021, under the supervision of <a href="https://sites.google.com/site/sivatheja/"  target="_blank" rel="noopener">Prof. Siva Theja Maguluri</a>.
